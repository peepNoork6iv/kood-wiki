# Income Sharing Agreement explained

Updated: June 10, 2026 3:53 PM

## Overview of the kood/Jõhvi Income Sharing Agreement

kood/Jõhvi operates as an NGO, is not actively governmentally funded and is supported by private donations to cover everyday running costs, it needs to be sustainable in alternative ways and to cover the costs of each student. Our Student Agreement also includes the Income Sharing Agreement, which ****applies to all the students that join kood/Jõhvi from 2023 onwards. The purpose of the fee is to cover kood/Jõhvi’s study and operational costs and helps to make the school sustainable for years to come and educate even more students each year.

**If a student gets a job in one of our partner companies**, then the Income Sharing Agreement does not apply. The partners are covering the fee by sponsoring the school. There are no extra fees or obligations included. **Each batch is assigned its own partner. To find out who your partner is, please reach out to the team at info@kood.tech**.

**If a student gets a job on their own,** the agreement comes into effect once the student starts to receive monthly gross income from their employer that is at least 1.3x the Estonian average income (based on the data from the statistics department). The students starting their studies in 2026 shall reimburse a total of €4500. Once the agreement comes into effect, the payment is not less than €200/month until the total sum is paid.

## Income Sharing Agreement - FAQ

- **What is the kood/Jõhvi Income Sharing Agreement?**
    
    The **Income Sharing Agreement** applies to all the students that join kood/Jõhvi from 2023 onwards. The purpose of the fee is to cover kood/Jõhvi’s study and operational costs and serves the purpose to make the school sustainable for years to come and educate even more students each year.
    
    **If a student gets a job in one of our [official partner companies](https://kood.tech/our-partners/)**, then the Income Sharing Agreement does not apply. The partners are covering the fee by sponsoring the school. There are no extra fees or obligations included.
    
    **If a student gets a job on their own,** the agreement comes into effect once the student starts to receive monthly gross income from their employer that is at least 1.3x the Estonian average income (based on the data from the statistics department on a 6-month average). The students starting their studies in 2023/24 shall reimburse a total of €4000. Once the agreement comes into effect, the payment is not less than €200/month until the total sum is paid.
    
- **Who is eligible for the Income Sharing Agreement?**
    
    All the students that join kood/Jõhvi from 2023 onwards have to sign an Income Sharing Agreement and reimburse a total of €4000 after they graduate or start working in a tech field while studying. 
    
- **How much do participants owe and when do they need to pay?**
    
    Participants owe a total of €4000 under the Income Sharing Agreement. They need to start making payments once they start earning a monthly gross income that is at least 1.3x the Estonian average income. The minimum monthly payment is €200.
    
- **What happens if a participant doesn't find a job?**
    
    If a participant doesn't find a job, they are not required to make any payments under the Income Sharing Agreement (ISA). However, they will need to provide salary statements every 6 months to demonstrate that they do not have a job or that their income is lower than the required threshold of 1.3 times the Estonian average income. The ISA remains valid for a duration of 10 years, during which time the participant's job status and income will be periodically reviewed to ensure compliance with the agreement's terms.
    
- **How long is the Income Sharing Agreement in effect?**
    
    The ISA remains valid for a duration of 10 years. After, the student is owed the total amount (€4000), the contract between us ends.
    
- **How is the Income Sharing Agreement different from a traditional loan?**
    
    The difference between ISA and a traditional loan is that ISA only requires payments to start once the participant secures a job and starts earning above a certain income threshold. Payments depend on the individual's earnings, making it more flexible and aligned with their financial capacity.
    
- **Can I just pay you €200 every month during the studies not after I finish the program?**
    
    If a student wishes to do so, there is the opportunity to pay €200 every month during their studies instead of paying in the end of the program.
    
- **If I become unemployed do I still have to pay you?**
    
    In that case, you can ask for a payment break, but the income-sharing obligation will last for 10 years. This means that until you pay us the entire amount, you’ll have to provide us with salary statements every 6 months to prove that you earn below 1.3x the Estonian average income. 
    
    After you pay us the total amount (€4000), you no longer have to provide any statements and the contract between us ends.
    
- **What are kood/Jõhvi’s partner companies?**
    
    You can find the list of our partner companies [here](https://kood.tech/en/about-kood/our-partners/). 
    
- **If I first get a job through kood/Jõhvi and later get a job on my own then will the Income Sharing Agreement still come into effect?**
    
    If you go to work at one of our partners companies (either through kood/Jõhvi) or their own application process, then you don’t have to worry about the Income Sharing Agreement. It’s important to note that this is valid if you successfully finish the probation period within the company (generally 4 months). If you or the company decides during the probation period not to proceed with the employment contract, then you are still going to be part of the Income Sharing Agreement model. The partners are covering the fee through sponsoring the school.
    
- **If I get just 1 paycheck that is above the 1.3x medium salary, will I immediately have to start paying the €200/month?**
    
    In this case, you pay for the period you were at work with the 1.3x average salary. After that you will start paying again when you get a job that also pays more than 1.3x the Estonian average salary.
    
- **Will I have to work for the partner for 2 years to make even the €4500 and if I leave earlier I still have to start paying the €200/per month?**
    
    You need to work at your batch’s partner company at least until the end of the Probationary Period (4 months) for the ISA to come into effect. From there, partner companies have deals with kood/Jõhvi to cover your financial commitments to kood/Jõhvi.
    
- **I am already working in a partner company, do I have to pay if I move to the engineering department within the company? (I am currently working in another department)**
    
    This conclusion will be discussed between you and the company. The outcome may depend on internal policies and agreements specific to your situation, so we recommend speaking directly with your employer.
    
- **I’m working during the studies in a non-tech related role but I earn more than the Minimum Income Threshold. When do I have to start paying?**
    
    If you are already earning more than the Minimum Income Threshold (1.3x Estonian average), then you will start paying back to the school either:
    
    a) when you switch your roles to tech-related roles (by contract you are obliged to inform the school when you take a new job in tech-related role)
    
    b) or when you graduate.
    
    Which ever situation happens first, this will be the starting point for the school to start tracking your salary. You also have the option to start paying back earlier if you don’t want the burden during your new role.
