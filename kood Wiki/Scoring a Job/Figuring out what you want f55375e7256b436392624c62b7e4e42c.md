# 💆Figuring out what you want

Start by reflecting on your **interests, strengths, and values.** 

- What are my **long-term aspirations** in the tech industry?
    - Think about your **dream job position 🚀** what type of responsibilities would you like to have, for example your career doesn’t have to progress into a managerial position, the end goal can easily be a senior specialist position. Importantly, there are **very many different IT positions**. I recommend researching various roles by looking at job ads around the world and finding the one that makes your eyes spark, and please, believe in yourself, don’t settle!
    - What type of skills would this position **require** from you? Think about both technical skills, and transferable/professional skills. **Evaluate which skills you already have and which you need to acquire**.
    - What do you need to do **to acquire the skills** you don’t have yet? Some of the skills are definitely covered in kood/Jõhvi workshops that take place throughout the year, so make sure to keep an eye on Discord events 🗓️👀
- **If you’re not ready to think so much ahead**, you can start small by answering the following questions:
    - What aspects of programming do I enjoy the most? 💃
    - What kind of problems do I find most interesting to solve? 🔍
    - What programming languages or technologies do I have a natural affinity for? 💕
- In any case, it’s important to think about **what kind of company** you would like to work for?
    - Think if the **values of the company align with your own values ✨** Most companies describe their values on their homepage, however, for a more reliable source reach out to someone working there or try to understand their key values during interview
    - Consider **other factors** about the organisation. Would you prefer a local or international, large or small, start-up or corporate, NGO, public or private sector organisation?
    - First find the company you would like to work at and since you’re convinced, the only thing left to do is to convince them!

**Practical exercise** to figure if a job/company is right for you ✍️
1. Take a piece of paper and mark down **12 things that are important** for you in a job (for example good pay, available tutors etc)
2. After your job interview **rate how the job/company is doing** on these aspects on a 1-10 scale. If the score is under 90, the fit is not that great.

Remember that to know what you want from your career means **knowing yourself as well**. It’s a life-long journey, and you can start, for example with [16Personalities test](https://www.16personalities.com/free-personality-test) 📝
