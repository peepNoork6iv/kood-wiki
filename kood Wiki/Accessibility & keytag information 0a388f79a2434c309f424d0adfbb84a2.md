# Accessibility & keytag information

Tags: Policies
Updated: March 2, 2026 1:05 PM

Each student is provided with their own private key chip that they can use to access the building **24/7**

<aside>
⚠️ **NB!** Keep your chip safe! Please note that you are personally responsible for this key and in case of loss or damage you must inform //kood either by mail (**info@kood.tech**) or in Discord (**write a ticket**) (New one will cost you **15€**)

</aside>

If you live in Student housing, access to your room is tied to your key. Access to the room is also possible with a 5-digit code. We renew the codes every 3 month and send them out by email.

<aside>
⚠️ **NB!** If you move out of the student housing, your key chip must be returned and you will be issued other chip for accessing the building

</aside>

Keys and codes can be obtained on weekdays from 10:00 to 16:00 by contacting housing@kood.tech
