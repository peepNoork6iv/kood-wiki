# 2023.08 [Batch 3]Questions about the Student Agreement and life at school

Tags: Batch 3, Onboarding, Student Agreement

- **Please clarify the information about the scholarship. Under what conditions can I apply for a scholarship? And how much is the scholarship will benefit?**
    
    We have a scholarship option. This is a monthly payment that is re-newed each 6 months. At the start of your studies, you need to apply by proving your motivation and need for the scholarship. The scholarships are re-visited after 6 months, and you need to keep a certain XP level compared to your batch to continue receiving the scholarship. If you have not achieved it, then other students who have reached the level but were not receiving a scholarship before, can apply for the next 6 months.
    
- **Hello! I have a question, when can we start moving in the dorm? Can we move in one day prior to the 13th when the school starts?**
    
    Move in day is 13th of September, unfortunately it is not possible to move in earlier.
    
- **Could you point out where's the FAQ answer "If a student gets a job through kood/Talent platform, then the ISA does not apply." stated in the Student Agreement?**
    
    Paragraph 3.6. "...In the event that a third party wishes to repay the Income Sharing Fee on behalf of the Student, such payment will be accepted and shall result in a corresponding reduction in the Student's obligations under this Agreement." If you get a job through kood/Jõhvi then the ISA is still paid, just by the company directly to us.

    Unfortunately, no. All the monitors we have are at use in the study building.

- I**s there a place where i can store my bicycle when i move in to the dorm?**
    
    There are bike racks in front of the school. You can also store your bike inside in under the staircase / basement floor.
    
- **What will the legal status of K/J students be if they study full-time? Will they be considered students as students of an institution of higher education?**
    
    Legally you have no status since we're not an official education institution. We're registered as a furthering education institution (täienduskoolitusasutus; EHIS ID: 50377). More info: [https://www.hm.ee/kutse-ja-taiskasvanuharidus/taiskasvanuharidus/taienduskoolitus](https://www.hm.ee/kutse-ja-taiskasvanuharidus/taiskasvanuharidus/taienduskoolitus) P.S. This also means that by law working people can take study leave for 30 calendar days during a calendar year to focus on studying.
    
    We're trying to work with the State in order to be an official education institution someday but currently, we don't fit into any criteria. Hopefully, with a lot of work and conversations with the public sector, we can get somewhere. But as always these things take time!
    
- **The study period is 20 months, but the fee is distributed evenly from 4-13 months. Could you please explain it in more detail and the logic behind it?**
    
    Thank you for pointing this out, it was meant to be 4-14 months, which would average €400 per month. The most important highlight here is that you can come and study for the first 3 months without any obligations - make sure to really test out if the study fits you and your lifestyle. If you have any hesitations within the first three months, please talk to the team or consider what’s the best option for you.
    
    If you do continue your studies but for whatever reason decide to leave on month 6, then there the Early Termination fee will apply. The fee is calculated from months 4-6, meaning 2 x €400 = €800. After the 14 months we do expect that majority of you will graduate and therefore the ISA model will apply. 
    
    The reason we have a fee is that we have made quite a lot of expenses on each student joining kood/Jõhvi, think: everything around selection sprint; laptops; the access to the platform; kood/team helping people; the beautiful building we have and so on. 
    
- **Agreement 8.2: Early termination fee can be changed to whatever amount (for no reason and with no limits) once a year (12 months). Cant it be fixed for 20month?**
    
    Thank you for pointing it out. This seems indeed a little unfair, we have adapted the agreement with a limit of maximum to 10% of increase of the total amount. However, so far there hasn’t been an increase to the previous batches and our goal is to keep the cost the same during the studies - so you know what you get into while you are studying. 
    
- **Do you have to pay the income sharing fee from the salary that one earns from whatever field of work or only from the salary earned in the IT-field?**
    
    Yes, exactly. By the end of the program whatever field you work in, if you earn more than 1.3x the Estonian average salary, then you have to pay the Income Sharing Fee.
    
- **Is it possible, if one would have financial difficulties, to pay the income sharing fee in parts smaller than 200€ using scheduled payments?**
    
    Yes, everything is possible if you talk it through with us.
    
- **Correct me if I'm wrong. When one would end studies on, for example, month number 9, than he/she would have to pay 5x200€ = 1000€?**
    
    The Early Termination Fee is distributed evenly from months 4-14 and the payment is then €400 for each month until the 14th month. 
    So if the individual decides to end studies after 9 months he/she would have to pay 5x400€ = 2000€. If the person decides to end studies on the 16th month then they pay the full 4000€ euros.
    
- **Can the cost of education change?**
    
    No, if you sign the agreement the cost of education can’t change it will stay as it is (4000€). Our ambition is to keep it at 4000€ but the “up to 10% per year” clause is put in the contract in case of extreme economic conditions.
    
- **Is it possible to suspend studies in case of a long illness?**
    
    Yes, it is possible, we understand that sometimes unexpected happen. Of course, you’ll have to contact the kood/Jõhvi team for the next steps in case of this situation.
    
- **Do we start paying the income sharing fee after the program, or do we have to start paying during the program if our salary exceeds 1,3x average salary?**
    
    If you get the job during your studies, you have an option to start paying the Income Sharing Fee straight away or wait until you finish the mandatory program and start paying then.
    
- **What level of education is given? Does it count towards some kind of state degree of study? How is the diploma quoted (in Estonia, in other countries)?**
    
    You will receive a Diploma after graduation that is accompanied by different certificates dependent on whether you finished more than the mandatory program.
    
    After you complete your studies, you do not receive an accredited degree as this educational model is new in Estonia and does not correspond to any traditional definitions. However, this is usually not a problem in the technology sector, given that companies tend to only care about what you can do, who you are, and how you work. This is also the spirit all our partner companies have.
    
- **3.3. what is meant by "to pay an amount of their gross income". Will the fee be more than 4000 if I earn more?**
    
    No, 4000€ is a fixed sum that does not change regardless of your income.
    
- **Do I need to provide any documents confirming my previous education?**
    
    No, we don’t require any documentation about your previous educational experience.
    
- **5.4. of agreement – How should I introduce the employer? What if he's not interested at all?**
    
    It is strongly recommended (not mandatory and there’s no way we can check) to explain kood/Jõhvi principles during job interviews that you might have with various employers in order to build awareness. The school is still very new and many employers are still not aware about the benefits and the study system of kood/Jõhvi. Of course over time, the hiring managers will get more acquainted with kood/ principles but this takes time- therefore the more you explain the principles, the more they understand the school, the more likely they are to hire you or somebody else from kood/.
    
- **What if I fail as a programmer and I will work, for example, as a builder. And my income will be more than in the agreement. Do I have to pay in this case?**
    
    By the end of the program whatever field you work in, if you earn more than 1.3x the Estonian average salary, then you have to pay the Income Sharing Fee.
    
- **8.3 of agrmnt «Fee shall be distributed evenly from months 4-14» Does this mean that if I studied for 13 months, then you already owe the entire amount of 4000?**
    
    Yes, if person decides to leave after 14 months of studying, then the entire amount of 4000€ would be owed. The reason for that is the direct costs for each student that we will already incur by that point. 
    
    Based on our experience, if you wish to leave the school, that desire could arise earlier than a year after. Thus, it is emphasized here that when coming to study, the individual makes an informed choice and knows the responsibility of paying back.
    
- **6 HEALTH INSURANCE. If I have only commertial insurans, Kood can give me health insurance coverage to the end of studing?**
    
    The school will provide health insurance to all the students who need it through a private provider.
    
- **Is it possible to get study leave from my work while being a student at kood/jõhvi?**
    
    Yes, you are eligible to get study leave (for 30 calendar days per calendar year) during your studies in kood/Jõhvi. We’ll provide you with the certificate proving that you are taking part in the studying process and that will allow you to request the study leave from your employer.
