# Office Hours sli.do (Batch 4)

Updated: September 11, 2025 8:29 PM

- **Batch1-4 study quality is really different. Is there a way to update old batch experience to new one?**
    
    As Jüri-Mikk discussed during the call, we acknowledge that our batches have varying learning experiences, more noticeably for batch4 with kood/Jõhvi’s own learning environment. Our goal has been and always will be to offer the most relevant and efficient learning experience we can, and we see each new batch as an opportunity to learn and implement changes to improve your overall experience. We’re currently weighing the options to update the experience of batch 1-3 and will get back to the students once we have an action plan.
    
- **Why didn’t you notify Batch3 people that Batch4 will have a new platform. The difference in experience of them both is immeasurable and seems unfair?**
    
    We communicated this out on Discord [on 10/26/23.](https://discord.com/channels/875324134530363395/876733142180966451/1167104672880918588) The difference in experience between Batch 3 and Batch 4 arises from the fact that Batch 4 is the first group to study on our newly developed system, while Batch 3 is learning on a system that has been used and tested many times. There are both positives and negatives to this difference. As a startup, which kood/Jõhvi is as a school, we are constantly experimenting and seeking the best solutions, which naturally means that different batches will have different experiences by design.
    
- **The GO block was discontinued, causing students to lose 6 months. This was known before income sharing agreement, will there be any comp for the lost time?**
    
    GO module is still available and mandatory for all batch 1-4 students, being the first module of the program. We did change Rust from a mandatory to an optional module, along with the rest of the specializations, making the curriculum last for minimum 3 modules total. Since the learning modules are still available to everyone, the content offered has not changed, and faster learners can complete the program in a year. If it is not possible to complete the program in 18 months, an extension can be requested. 
    
- **If there’s only 1 office hour participant. Maybe the problem is in office hours not the students?**
    
    There were 37 participants in this Office Hours participating online. We believe that the office hours format works since people can come and ask their questions openly. Also, we recorded the session for people who couldn’t attend live, so everybody will have accessibility to the information afterwards.
    
- **Why does batch 3 have to pay when batch 1 and 2 did not, and we have the same product?**
    
    kood/Jõhvi operates as an non-profit organisation with the mission to help to reduce the lack of software engineers in Estonia, offer an innovative addition to regular schools and strengthen the current educational system. Batch 1 was the first trial with this model; we didn't know if it would work, if people would still be able to learn the profession this way, and if they would succeed in the job market. Today, we know that this approach works.
    
    Since 2021, our operations have been funded by donations from partners and supporters, including companies that also offer jobs to our alumni. To ensure future growth and sustainability, we’ve introduced an Income Sharing Agreement to help cover study costs and admit new students. Initially, we relied on recruitment fees from companies, but challenges like the COVID-19 pandemic, the war in Ukraine, and ongoing economic downturns have highlighted the need for student contributions. Thus, we had to adapt by either closing the school or seeking contributions from successful students to support its operations.
    
- **Are the recruiters as positive about learning java in 2-3 weeks when you have Go listed even if they have 20 people with 1y of Java experience on table?**
    
    For recruiters it's not about the time spent learning Java, but more about what skills you have gained and how you can demonstrate your abilities in Java, meaning if you can show or technically describe projects you've completed in Java, it doesn't matter how much time you spent. If you are talking about university graduates here, I wouldn't worry too much because during that year they have various courses with little emphasis on the practical, it's not like they learn Java 40h a week. And our students with 0 Java have taken roles with Java from university graduates, so it doesn't seem to be the primary importance for the recruiters. From another perspective, there are ~100 kood/Jõhvi graduates that have gotten a job in the ICT sector, either in Java or other non-Go languages. We recommend talking to these people and understanding what worked (and didn’t work) for them.
    
- **If some advertised claims turned out to be false after applying to K/J, can the income sharing agreement be voided?**
    
    Every Batch 3 student has had the opportunity to experience the product for free during the selection sprint (3 weeks) and during the trial period (3 months) in total for almost 4 months before any commitments to pay were triggered. This gave ample opportunity to evaluate by your own personal experience if the program is suitable and meets your expectations (which might or might not have been shaped by advertisements). The study agreement is designed to be straightforward, and we went over the main points during the student info meeting after the selection sprint. This was to make sure everyone felt comfortable and informed before joining the batch. Once you've signed the Student Agreement, the Income Sharing Agreement is in place and can't be voided.
    
- **How do you think, did this meeting go well?**
    
    Of course! We got to spend some time with you guys, and hopefully this meeting helped to clarify some of the questions you may have had and update you on what’s going on under the hood. If you have any further questions or suggestions, do let us know and we will be ready to improve this format according to your needs.
