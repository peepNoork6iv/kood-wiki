**Student Housing**

- kood/Võru has a total of 20 spots in the dormitory.
- Each room accommodates 2-4 people in bunk beds or regular beds.
- The rooms include closet space and a table with chairs.
- There is one shared kitchen on the dormitory's second floor with a sink, fridge, stove, and other equipment. A laundry room is also available.
- You can rent a bed at the kood/Võru dormitory:
    - Monthly: **55-80 EUR** (+ VAT)
    - Nightly (if space available): **8-12** EUR per night (+ VAT)
- For all housing-related questions, contact: housing@kood.tech

**HOUSE RULES FOR STUDENT HOUSING AT KREUTZWALDI 16, VÕRU**

This annex sets forth the internal rules and obligations that apply to all residents of the student housing located at Kreutzwaldi 16, Võru. These rules are binding and form an integral part of the Student Housing Agreement. By signing the Agreement, the resident confirms that they have read, understood, and agree to comply with these rules.

**1. General Responsibilities**

1.1. Residents are required to behave respectfully and responsibly toward other residents, staff, and visitors.

1.2. Student housing is for registered residents only. Overnight guests are not permitted.

1.3. Quiet hours are in effect from 23:00 to 08:00. Noise that may disturb others is not allowed during this time.

1.4. The premises are intended solely for residential use.

**2. Cleanliness and Maintenance**

2.1. Residents are responsible for cleaning and maintaining their assigned rooms.

2.2. Shared spaces (e.g. kitchens, bathrooms, hallways) must be left clean and tidy after use.

2.3. Cleaning equipment (vacuum cleaners, mops, etc.) is available in the designated storage area and must be cleaned and returned after use.

2.4. Personal belongings, food waste, and unwashed dishes must not be left in common areas.

**3. Kitchen and Food Storage**

3.1. Residents must use their own cooking utensils, dishes, and cleaning products.

3.2. All items stored in the shared refrigerator must be clearly labeled with the resident’s name and room number.

3.3. Residents must clean all kitchen surfaces, stovetops, and sinks after use.

3.4. Food waste must not be disposed of in sinks or toilets.

**4. Bathroom and Shower Facilities**

4.1. Shower and bathroom areas must be kept clean and dry after use.

4.2. The shower facilities are also used by the clients of the sports center between 08:00 and 20:00. Students are requested to use the showers preferably when they are not occupied and to be considerate of shared use during these hours.

**5. Laundry Facilities**

5.1. Washing machines and dryers are available on the ground floor for use by residents.

5.2. Residents must bring their own detergent and follow a first-come, first-served principle when using the laundry facilities.

5.3. Dryer filters and water containers must be emptied after each use.

5.4. Machines must not be moved or tampered with.

**6. Room Assignment and Condition**

6.1. Assigned rooms are personal and must not be shared or sublet.

6.2. Long-term residents may leave their belongings in the room during their stay.

6.3. Short-term residents must clean their room and bed area before departure.

6.4. Any damage must be reported to housing@kood.tech.

6.5. Residents may personalize their space only with non-damaging decorations. Attaching items to walls, furniture or ceilings is not permitted.

**7. Access and Security**

7.1. Access to rooms is provided via physical keys; building entry requires a card or code.

7.2. Sharing or duplicating keys or codes is strictly prohibited.

7.3. Lost access tools must be reported immediately to housing@kood.tech.

7.4. Security cameras operate at the building’s main entrances.

7.5. The building is partially accessible for residents with mobility needs (first floor only).

**8. Shared Facilities and Internet**

8.1. Communal items (e.g. board games) must be returned to their original place after use, so they remain accessible to everyone.

8.2. Wi-Fi is available throughout the building. Network details will be provided separately.

**9. Prohibited Conduct**

The following are strictly prohibited:

a. Smoking or vaping anywhere indoors or on school property;

b. Consumption or possession of alcohol on the premises;

c. Use, possession, or distribution of illegal substances;

d. Intentional damage to property;

e. Use of another resident’s belongings without permission;

f. Blocking, removing, or tampering with safety equipment (e.g. smoke detectors);

g. Harassment, threats, or disrespectful behavior towards others.

**10. Violations and Disciplinary Measures**

10.1. Any rule violation will result in a written warning via email.

10.2. After a second written warning, the resident must vacate the premises within 24 hours.

10.3. In case of serious misconduct or criminal offense, immediate removal without warning may be enforced.

**11. Contact and Communication**

11.1. For housing-related issues (maintenance, questions, reports), contact: housing@kood.tech

11.2. There is no on-site security staff in Võru. All issues must be directed to the housing team: housing@kood.tech.

These rules are binding. Non-compliance may result in the loss of housing privileges or termination of the housing agreement.

# ‼️**And more important factors…**

- **Respect each other, please!** The number one unwritten rule for living with other people is **Respect!** Basically, it implies these common-sense rules for a nice coexistence.
- **Do not make noise between 11 PM and 8 AM.** This actually goes without saying, but the silent mode is a good idea! Common areas are meant for hanging out, having phone conversations, or other activities. Use them and do not disturb your roommates.
- **Want to cook? Clean up afterward.** The kitchen is a common area, please do your dishes right after cooking and keep it clean for everyone.
- **Use trash bins.**  No food down the sinks, please!
- **The bathroom is not your SPA.** The bathroom is also a common area, please consider others.
- **A note for Early and Late birds:** If you arrive at the hostel very late/early, just prepare all the stuff you will need outside of the room before you go in. That way, you won’t disturb your roommates.
- **Tag it! (your food)** Before putting your food in the fridge or on shelves simply tag your food with your name and room number.
- **If you move it, put it back.**
- **Drinking alcohol and smoking and vaping is forbidden indoors and school premises!**
- **Don’t act against the applicable laws, rules, and regulations of the Republic of Estonia.**
- **A smile goes a long way (in a hostel and anywhere).**
