# Studying at //kood

- **When will I know if I got into kood/Jõhvi, kood/Järva or kood/Võru?**
    
    We will start finalizing the results and letting the people accepted know after the end of the Selection Sprint. It can take up to 2 weeks.
    
- **Do I need to sign a contract with kood/Jõhvi to start studying?**
    
    Yes, all students who start studying at kood/Jõhvi sign a Student Agreement that covers the rights and obligations of both the student and the school. You have the right to terminate the agreement within 3 months from the start of your studies without any obligations. If you continue studying and decide to leave between month 4 and graduating, you are obliged to pay the Termination Fee in order to cover the expenses that are made per student (detailed information in the contract which you will receive once accepted to the school). 
    
    As the school operates as a NGO and is not governmentally funded, it needs to be sustainable in alternative ways and to cover the costs of each student. The agreement also includes the **Income Sharing Agreement** applies to all the students that join kood/Jõhvi from 2023 onwards. The purpose of the fee is to cover kood/Jõhvi’s study and operational costs and serves the purpose to make the school sustainable for years to come and educate even more students each year.
    
    **If a student gets a job through our kood/Talent platform**, then the Income Sharing Agreement does not apply. The companies that hire the talents through kood/Talent will pay recruitment fees which cover the costs of the student. There are no extra fees or obligations included.
    
    **If a student gets a job on their own,** the agreement comes into effect once the student starts to receive monthly gross income from their employer that is at least 1.3x the Estonian average income (based on the data from the statistics department on 6 month average). The students starting their studies in 2023/24 shall reimburse a total of €4000. Once the agreement comes into effect, the payment is not less than €200/month until the total sum is paid.
    
- **What does the study program consist of?**
    
    kood/Jõhvi 2-year program consists of 3 (2+1) compulsory modules + 1 optional module. The new program gives all the needed skills to have a successful career in the ICT sector. 
    **Go** and **JavaScript** are compulsory modules that need to be completed, the third module can be **Rust** or **at least one specialisation module**. You have certain deadlines by what you need to complete these modules according to the program. 
    
    **If you want to complete an optional module** (either Rust or a different specialisation) then you get an extra Certificate that showcases your acquired skills. You can complete different specialisations as some of them take less time than others and they are quite individual.
    
- **Will I get a diploma or a degree after the completion of the study?**
    
    You will receive a Certificate after graduation that is accompanied by different certificates dependent on if you finished more than the mandatory program.
    
    After you complete your studies, you do not receive an accredited degree as this educational model is new in Estonia and does not correspond to any traditional definitions. However, we do not believe this to be a problem in the technology sector, given that companies tend to only care about what you can do, who you are, and how you work.
    
- **How similar will be the Sprint and the actual studies? How intensive the 2-year program will be? How tight are deadlines for quests and tasks? Same as in this sprint or less intensive?**
    
    Generally, the program will be less intense compared to the Selection Sprint, but you will still have deadlines that you need to follow. 
    
    The idea of the Selection Sprint is really to test you to see how you cope with harsh deadlines and tricky tests, while the program is focusing on your constant development at your own speed. 
    
    The deadlines in the rest of the program are usually weeks, not days, but you will still have two Sprints during the two years that will be more intense. However, the longer deadlines do not mean that you have a lot of free time, the tasks are more extensive and have other smaller tasks in them, so you definitely need to put some time into them. 
    
- **Can the program be done mostly remotely? Let’s say 90/10 ratio of remote/on-site work?**
    
    Based on the experience, then it is recommended to be on-site as much as possible as it is easier to study together with others (which you hopefully have learned at the Selection Sprint). However, it is possible to do it also remotely, but it will definitely be harder. We recommend being on-site as much as possible and cannot give the exact percentage of time that you could do remotely. It also depends on your own personal time management skills, motivation, working possibilities at home etc. The key thing is not to fall behind and actually learn and that is definitely easier on-site.  
    
    There will also be additional events, hackathons etc on-site that are mandatory to attend on-site in order to complete your studies. 
    
- **If 80% of the projects are group projects, then how can one go through the program at their own pace? Especially in cases where groups are randomly assigned?**
    
    If you reach teamwork and there are no other students at the same level, you have other optional tasks to continue with to strengthen your knowledge and learn in more detail about a certain coding language or task.
    You can form your own teams / groups and these are **not done** randomly.
    
- **Do you have to submit & succeed in a task (within a Quest) in order to see the next task (similarly to the Sprint)?**
    
    During the school year, there's a combination of access. Some quests are open simultaneously, some work the same way as you had during the Selection Sprint (submit to receive access to the next one). Generally, you have longer deadlines compared to the Sprint. The idea of the Selection Sprint is really to test you, during the study program itself it is up to you to gain all the knowledge and skills at your own speed. 
    
- **Will it be possible to progress through exams faster than normal? Or will they happen at the same time for everyone, like in the Sprint?**
    
    There are no mandatory exams in the program, but you will have 2 additional Sprints (JavaScript and Rust/Specialization) and it will be possible to progress to them faster if you have done all the pre-requisite tasks beforehand. 
    
- **What will be the grading/passing system at the school?**
    
    You will not receive grades, but XPs similar to the Selection Sprint. There are mandatory tasks you need to complete to gain access to the next ones and also optional and harder ones that you can do if you want to. You need to complete all the mandatory ones to graduate the school. 
    
- **What if I fall behind with my studies?**
    
    You will have specific deadlines that you need to complete to get XPs during the program and in some cases, you need to complete them to get access to the next tasks. We will keep track of everyone's performance to see if anyone is falling behind and needing help, but definitely, a good way to avoid that is to be on spot at the school as it's easier to ask for help from your peers and to catch up. 
    
- **Will there be real-life meetings with industry reps etc. during the 2-year program?**
    
    There will be industry meetings, hackathons, and other events with our partners and industry players. We are also planning on additional courses in 'soft-skills' to help you in the job market (e.g. building your resume, how to succeed in a job interview etc). 
    
- **During the Sprint our tasks were controlled by autotests, how will it be during the main curriculum?**
    
    During the main curriculum the main projects are evaluated on peer-to-peer basis aka **audits**. During the main curriculum you will also have **Sprints** in the start of new tracks, which also have autotests
    
- **If i start in January, i assume that i will not be able to team up with September batch. Will the January pool be smaller and/or how will the 2 batches mix?**
    
    The community reaches outside the borders and all events etc. are usually done together with other batches. Studying happens together with your own batch.
    
- **I'm about to finish my studies. What do I do? How do I get my certificate?**
    
    If you completed the three required modules, congratulations, you did it! Now you need to write us an email to **students@kood.tech** with information about your **first name, last name, gitname**. Then our team will confirm the fact that you have completed all the required tasks and send you a confirmation email. You will receive a digital certificate of training after you return the rented equipment (computer, monitor) **NB!** You will need to return the equipment within **two weeks** after receiving the letter of successful completion of training. Hardware could be returned either by courier, omniva/smartpost parcel machines or directly on-spot (at the admins desk). Number for the courier or parcel machines: +37258400917. Address for the courier is 2.Tartu põik 5, 41537 (Or Omniva, Smartpost in Grossi store)
    

## School building & dorms

- **How much does it cost to live in the dorms and how can I book a spot? How do you decide who gets them if there will be more demand than free places?**
    - Yes! You can rent a bed at kood/Jõhvi dormitory (monthly rate per bed is 60 EUR)
    - If there’s a free space, the beds can be rented by night, 5 EUR per night for our students and alumni. Price for people outside of our organisation is 10 EUR per night.
    - Rooms are distributed on 1st come, 1st served logic. **You can sign up for a room via form or e-mailing housing@kood.tech.** Information will be sent to your email. You need to fill in the form and pay first month rent.
- **Do I have to live on campus?**
    
    No, living on campus is not mandatory, however, we strongly recommend living in close proximity to the school. This ensures that you will be able to collaborate with other students, create meaningful bonds, and succeed in your studies.
    
- **Is there enough room in the dorm rooms to bring my desktop computer?**
    
    You can setup your desktop in the co-working space. The dorm rooms have been set up so to use them for sleeping / chilling not studying.
    
- **Is it possible to move into a dorm room with pre-chosen roommates, provided there are enough empty slots in a room?**
    
    Yes!
    

## Income Sharing Agreement (Batch 1-3)

- **What is the kood/Jõhvi Income Sharing Agreement?**
    
    The **Income Sharing Agreement** applies to all the students that join kood/Jõhvi from 2023 onwards. The purpose of the fee is to cover kood/Jõhvi’s study and operational costs and serves the purpose to make the school sustainable for years to come and educate even more students each year.
    
    **If a student gets a job in one of our [official partner companies](https://kood.tech/our-partners/)**, then the Income Sharing Agreement does not apply. The partners are covering the fee by sponsoring the school. There are no extra fees or obligations included.
    
    **If a student gets a job on their own,** the agreement comes into effect once the student starts to receive monthly gross income from their employer that is at least 1.3x the Estonian average income (based on the data from the statistics department on a 6-month average). The students starting their studies in 2023/24 shall reimburse a total of €4000. Once the agreement comes into effect, the payment is not less than €200/month until the total sum is paid.
    
- **Who is eligible for the Income Sharing Agreement?**
    
    All the students that join kood/Jõhvi from 2023 onwards have to sign an Income Sharing Agreement and reimburse a total of €4000 after they graduate or start working in a tech field while studying. 
    
- **How much do participants owe and when do they need to pay?**
    
    Participants owe a total of €4000 under the Income Sharing Agreement. They need to start making payments once they start earning a monthly gross income that is at least 1.3x the Estonian average income. The minimum monthly payment is €200.
    
- **What happens if a participant doesn't find a job?**
    
    If a participant doesn't find a job, they are not required to make any payments under the Income Sharing Agreement (ISA). However, they will need to provide salary statements every 6 months to demonstrate that they do not have a job or that their income is lower than the required threshold of 1.3 times the Estonian average income. The ISA remains valid for a duration of 10 years, during which time the participant's job status and income will be periodically reviewed to ensure compliance with the agreement's terms.
    
- **How long is the Income Sharing Agreement in effect?**
    
    The ISA remains valid for a duration of 10 years. After you pay us the total amount (€4000), the contract between us ends.
    
- **How is the Income Sharing Agreement different from a traditional loan?**
    
    The difference between ISA and a traditional loan is that ISA only requires payments to start once the participant secures a job and starts earning above a certain income threshold. Payments depend on the individual's earnings, making it more flexible and aligned with their financial capacity.
    
- **What are your partner companies?**
    
    You can find the list of our partner companies [here](https://kood.tech/our-partners/). 
    
- **Can I just pay you €200 every month during the studies not after I finish the program?**
    
    If a student wishes to do so, there is the opportunity to pay €200 every month during their studies instead of paying in the end of the program.
    
- **If I first get a job through kood/Jõhvi and later get a job on my own then will the Income Sharing Agreement still come into effect?**
    
    If you go to work at one of our partner companies (either through kood/Jõhvi) or their own application process, then you don’t have to worry about the Income Sharing Agreement. It’s important to note that this is valid if you successfully finish the probation period within the company (generally 4 months). If you or the company decides during the probation period not to proceed with the employment contract, then you are still going to be part of the Income Sharing Agreement model. The partners are covering the fee by sponsoring the school.
    
- **If I get just 1 paycheck that is above the 1.3x medium salary, will I immediately have to start paying the €200/month?**
    
    In this case, you pay for the period you were at work with the 1.3x average salary. After that you will start paying again when you get a job that also pays more than 1.3x the Estonian average salary.
    
- **Will I have to work for the partner for 2 years to make even the €4000 and if I leave earlier I still have to start paying the €200/per month?**
    
    You need to work at the partner company at least until the end of the Probationary Period (4 months) for the ISA to come into effect. From there, partner companies have deals with kood/Jõhvi to cover your financial commitments to kood/Jõhvi.
    
- **I am already working in a partner company, do I have to pay if I move to the engineering department within the company? (I am currently working in another department)**
    
    If you already work in a partner company before coming to kood/Jõhvi and get a promotion there (change the position/department) through the knowledge and skills you acquired in our school, then you don’t have to pay. Please note, that you still have to let us know about the change of your position in a company.
    

## Extra questions from Batch 1-3

- **Describe clearly the process if I will leave the school in the middle of the program. What costs will I have to pay?**
    
    After getting accepted we have a Student Agreement that allows you to study and try the program with no fees for the first 3 months of the program. For Batch 3 and 4 - If you leave after that then you need to pay an early termination fee that is counted according to the months studied in the program (months 4-14). For example, if you decide to leave on month 6, that means you have to pay back 3x €400 = €1200. If you leave in month 8, then you pay back 5 x €400 = €2000. 
    
    *For Batch 1 & 2: the fee is distributed between months 4-24. For example, you decide to leave on month 6, that means you have to pay back 2x €200 = €400.* 
    
- **Do you reset our XP to 0 after the sprint?**
    
    Yes!
    
- **What kind of financial support options would new batch students have available to them (from kood/Jõhvi or elsewhere)? What are the requirements?**
    
    We have a scholarship option. This is a monthly payment that is re-newed each 6 months. At the start of your studies, they are motivation and need based as you progress, receiving the scholarship will be connected to your study results. The exact conditions will be communicated to you! 
    
- **If I want to study remotely, what kind of technology does the school provide?**
    
    Laptop, monitor, mouse, mousepad and keyboard. If needed.
    
- **Is it correct, that kood/Jõhvi students cannot apply for a student loan?**
    
    Since we’re registered in Estonia as a furthering education institution then unfortunately by the law you can’t apply for a student loan at the moment. 
    
    But you do have the possibility to get up to 30 days of study leave from work [as stated in the law](https://www.hm.ee/et/eesmargid-tegevused/taiskasvanuharidus/oppepuhkus-ja-koolituse-rahastamine). 
    
- **What options will there be (paid or not) for short-term accommodation during events/hackathons/Sprints?**
    
    If there’s a free space in the dormitory, the beds can be rented by night for 5 EUR per night.
    
- **Can I apply for a study leave while studying in kood/Jõhvi?**
    
    Since kood/Jõhvi is registered as a furthering education institution according to EHIS (Estonian Education Info System) then you can apply for paid study leave for 30 days a year according to the law.
    
    [https://www.hm.ee/kutse-ja-taiskasvanuharidus/taiskasvanuharidus/oppepuhkus-ja-koolituse-rahastamine](https://www.hm.ee/kutse-ja-taiskasvanuharidus/taiskasvanuharidus/oppepuhkus-ja-koolituse-rahastamine)
    
    If you need an official letter of confirmation, write to info@kood.tech. 
    
- **If you end up opening schools in other cities, are current /Jõhvi students welcome to use the rooms or will they be reserved only for students in that branch?**
    
    Of course you’re welcome. The idea is to do hubs where you can get to know new people etc. It’s a big community! Maybe in the future even in other countries… Stay tuned! kood/Järva (Keskväljak 15, Paide) and kood/Võru (Tartu tn 25), Võru  will be opened in September 2025.
    
- **Do we get any kind of material after Sprint to keep in touch or study more?**
    
    We will keep the Discord open for at least a year so you can keep in touch with each other. 
    
- **Can the projects that I’ve done in school be shared online?**
    
    No.
    

# Health Insurance

- **Can apply to health insurance any time during the //kood program?**
    
    Yes, if you don’t have a governmental health insurance. Proof of no health insurance must be sent to students@kood.tech.
    
- **Is Confido health insurance the same as Haigekassa or not?**
    - It is not same, there is a certain percentage that you will need to pay for the services. Best alternative to the governmental insurance is not available.
    - This means that you will have a set budget and own financial responsibility for different activities that require a doctor.
- **Does your health insurance match to the condition for the required health insurance to apply the residence permit? Can we see the detail of the coverage?**
    
    Since the health insurance conditions are subject to change by Confido then we can’t give you an answer. We can send you the current conditions via email and you can check if it matches the conditions. Write to info@kood.tech
