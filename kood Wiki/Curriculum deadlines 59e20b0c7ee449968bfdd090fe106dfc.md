# Batch 3

We highly value your autonomy and understand that everyone's learning pace is different. That's why we've designed our courses to be flexible—there are no fixed class schedules!

### **On Your Own Time ⏰**

Feel free to plan your own study time, dig deeper into topics that intrigue you, and set a pace that aligns with your personal and professional commitments. Self-discipline is your best friend here!

### **Important Deadlines 🗓️**

While the journey is flexible, it's crucial to keep in mind the major milestones we have. Each student is expected to complete the following three modules by their respective deadlines:

1. Go-Module (aka forum is passed): **Due 20.03.24**
2. JavaScript Module (aka bomberman-dom is passed): **Due 02.10.24**
3. Specialization Module (Choose from Cybersecurity, AI, DevOps, etc.): **Due 25.04.25**

These deadlines are firm and non-negotiable. Missing a deadline will affect your course completion status and may require administrative action.

### Piscines 🤽🏽‍♂️

Before taking on a new module there are piscines (or sprints) which are three to four weeks of intensive learning dedicated to acquiring the basics of a specific language. These are mandatory parts of the curriculum and prepare you for the upcoming module.

You can’t initialise them on your own these therefore there are two main JavaScript sprints planned for you. 

- JS-Piscine 1: **08.01.24 - 28.01.24**
- JS-Piscine 2: **26.02.24 - 17-03.24**

If there are people who speedrun through the program then there will be an even earlier event as well for them, but it will be planned according to needs.

### **Requirements for piscines 🤓:**

For starting with piscines we have some tasks requirements, to ensure step-by-step learning and to make studying process more structured.

- **js-piscine** — go-module forum task finished or at least started. It is recommended to have the forum task finished (i.e. to have all audits received) and to do some independent research on js beforehand. 
**Note** that if you start piscine without finishing go-module forum task (i.e. did’t receive all the audits), then upon successful js-piscine completion the js-module will be instantly available (but you still have to finish forum to have go-module finished)
- **specialisation-piscine** — js-module bomberman-dom task finished or at least started. It is recommended to have the bomberman-dom finished (i.e. to have all audits received). 
**Note** that if you start piscine without finishing js-module bomberman-dom task (i.e. didn’t receive all the audits), then upon successful specialisation-piscine completion the specialisation-module will be instantly available (but you still have to finish bomberman-dom to have js-module finished)
- **ux-piscine** and **ui-piscine** — have js-module started :) there are limitations on the platform for registering to these piscines, so usually there will be a call for registration on Discord, so we can add you to the piscine manually. 
**Note** that to start UX/UI module you have to finish both UX and UI piscines, the order of completion doesn’t matter, i.e. you can choose which one to do first.
