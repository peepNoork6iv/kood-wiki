**Academic Leave Policy**

*Academic leave is a break from your studies that temporarily pauses your enrolment. You can take academic leave for various reasons (health issues, personal matters, financial challenges, work obligations, or other circumstances that prevent you from concentrating on your studies).*

*During this period, you remain registered in the school, but you do not have access to the platform, cannot submit tasks, and are not expected to participate in study-related activities.*

🚨 **Highlights**

- Academic leave can be taken once during the programme 1️⃣
- Maximum academic leave period is **6 months** 🗓️
- During academic leave, access to the platform, task submissions, and reviews are suspended 🙅‍♀️
- No additional fee applies for taking academic leave 💸
- Depending on curriculum compatibility, students may be transferred to another batch when returning ↔️ But cannot be transferred to Batch 9 due to curriculum differences ⚠️ meaning Students from Batch 6 may continue studies in Batch 7 or Batch 8, Batch 7 to Batch 8. But if no suitable batch is available, studies must continue individually 👤
- Students are strongly advised to complete any ongoing tasks before starting academic leave. Unfinished tasks may need to be restarted after returning to studies 🔄

**Academic Leave in Detail**

If you apply for academic leave, your study period is “frozen”, meaning you will retain the remaining study time you had at the moment the leave started.

For example, if you apply for academic leave 6 months before the end of your programme, upon returning you will still have those remaining 6 months to complete your studies.

**Important Note About Ongoing Tasks**

Students are strongly encouraged to complete any active tasks before beginning academic leave. If a task remains unfinished at the start of academic leave, curriculum or platform changes during the leave period may require the student to restart the task after returning to studies.

**Returning After Academic Leave**

The procedure for returning to studies after academic leave must be discussed with the Study Administrator via email at students@kood.tech.

Due to curriculum updates introduced starting from Batch 9, returning students may not always be placed into the newest batch. Placement depends on curriculum compatibility and available study groups.

- Students from Batch 6 may continue studies in Batch 7 or Batch 8 if applicable. Students from Batch 7 may continue studies in Batch 8 if applicable.
- Transfer to Batch 9 is not possible due to significant curriculum differences.
- If no suitable batch is available, the student will continue studies individually.

Students continuing individually may still use Discord and communicate with peers for support and advice; however, group-based task completion will not be available.

## **Early Termination During Academic Leave**

### **Batches 5 till 8**

If a student applies for academic leave but later decides not to rejoin the programme, an Early Termination Fee may apply.

For example:

- If a student applies for academic leave during the 4th month of studies, the trial period (months 1–2) has already passed.
- The full programme fee (€4000) is distributed across months 3–12.
- The Early Termination Fee is calculated as €400 per remaining study month.

Therefore, in this example, the applicable termination fee would be €800.

If academic leave is taken during the trial period and the student informs //kood before the end of the academic leave that they will not continue, no termination fee will apply.

**FAQ**

**Can I return to studies before my academic leave ends?**

Yes. It is possible to resume studies before the planned end date of academic leave.

In such cases, your study period resumes with the same remaining study time you had when the academic leave started.

To restart your studies, contact students@kood.tech.

Please note: responses may take up to 5 working days. The team does not respond outside working hours, on weekends, or on public holidays.
