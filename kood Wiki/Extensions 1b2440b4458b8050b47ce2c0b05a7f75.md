**EXTENSIONS**

**Module extension**

Student has the right to ask for an extension once for each module. The extension cannot be longer than 1 month. 

The extension **will not extend** the overall study period, it only extends the module deadline inside the study period. Except for the specialization module, the extension extends the studies by one month.

To get an extension a student must send a digitally signed document to [students@kood.tech](mailto:students@kood.tech)  **before** the module original deadline. The document must include the new deadline (cannot be more than one month after the original deadline), the reason for extension and a timeline for the uncompleted tasks. **Template for the document can be found [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1DA1UJdrp1RlFfWpxZLHEa8JDvCOKzZB5xCazh4Z0DX8/edit?tab=t.0)** (for extensions within a month after the deadline)

<aside>
⚠️

If the original module deadline comes and a student has not finished the module or asked for an extension, they will be removed from the module. 

</aside>

**According to the Student Agreement 7.3 (Batch 1-2) and 7.5 (Batch 3-5) **If the deadlines are not met, then kood/Jõhvi will send a warning after which a student has 14 days to improve.**

**If there is no improvement after 14 days, kood/Jõhvi has a right to:**
- take away student scholarships;
- terminate the student agreement, whereas the Early Termination Fees would still apply.

**Extension of studies**

To get an extension after the last day of studies a student must ask for an Annex for their Student Agreement. To do so the student has to send a digitally signed document to [students@kood.tech](mailto:students@kood.tech). The document must include the new deadline, the reason for extension and a timeline for the uncompleted tasks, **template for the document can be found [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1GMQDpGQMixqh1bT0vJe3m0_9TkVfkcCX7diKkxAeC6c/edit?usp=sharing)** (only for €200/month extensions).

If the Student does not meet the last day of studies deadline they can continue their studies only if they start paying the Study Extension Fee. Study Extension Fee is 200€ per month for the extended time.
