# 👥 //kood Community Guidelines

//kood is a new-age coding school that brings together like-minded people who share the passion for developing their technological skills.

//kood is not a solo project. It’s based on peer-to-peer learning and therefore centers around working together with other students. 

The //kood team acts as a supporting mechanism for the students throughout the 12-18 months study process. 

### **Our values:**

- **Support and collaboration** by being supportive of each other, engaging in peer-to-peer learning and collaboration, and recognizing the strength of working together.
- **Growth and learning** focusing on being growth-oriented, learning by doing, and viewing failure as an integral part of the learning process. This includes the development of both soft and hard skills.
- **Trust** by valuing independence built on trust, while also advocating for honesty and the courage to experiment and do things differently.
- **Inclusivity and accessibility** by creating an inclusive and welcoming space where IT education is accessible to all interested individuals and where everyone feels their opinion matters.
- **Courage** to experiment, do things differently and give honest feedback.

Please read the Guidelines below. Or gather together to read through it. These are meant to be followed by everyone involved in kood/Jõhvi, so you should know the content.

⭐ **Also, make sure you understand the contents of the ‘[Code of Conduct](https://intra.kood.tech/codeOfConduct)’ and Student Agreement.**

# 💚 **Expectations for //kood students**

- **Open communication**. Everyone can share their opinions and thoughts in a safe space, but stay open-minded and empathetic toward different responses and opinions.
- **Respect the people around you** and embrace differences. One of the great things about the kood/Jõhvi community is that it draws in a variety of people, so let’s hold respect for one another.
- **Be kind**. **Give and receive.** Peer-to-peer learning is just about that. People working together to evolve! The more insights, ideas, and thoughts are passed around, the merrier.
- **Share your ideas.** Feel like something’s missing? Or something else? Come to the kood/Jõhvi team and let’s find a way to make your idea into a reality.
- **Respect the space.** Leave it as you find it. If you moved things around then return them to their original location and **please tidy up after yourself**.
- **Please be sustainable** - turn off the lights if you are not using them; recycle bins, bottles, cans, etc. Use reusable water bottles and mugs.
- **Practice good personal hygiene and clean up your spaces**, especially in common areas. This is common knowledge but just something to think about.
- **Join the events.** If you sign up for the events, it means you have to show up or give a heads-up at least 24 hours beforehand if you can not join. If you have missed two events without letting us know and canceling your attendance at the last minute without good cause then take into account that we will take further steps into action. For example, this might be removing the opportunity to take part in events for a certain time period.

## The Don’ts

- Don't damage or deface kood/Jõhvi or its fixtures, décor, or furnishings.
- **Don’t do drugs! The same goes for alcohol, especially within the school premises. It is strictly prohibited and police will be involved if needed.**
- Don’t break the web, servers, devices, or passwords or do other mean things.
- Don’t download or send anything that could break the Internet, or someone’s devices or property. Nor anything you wouldn’t want your mother to see.
- Don’t steal stuff! Using someone else’s physical or intellectual property is considered stealing. And stealing is a punishable crime. We will act accordingly.
- **Don’t smoke/vape indoors or on the school premises.** It has been restricted in Estonia already from 2005.
- Don’t act against the applicable laws, rules, and regulations of the Republic of Estonia.
- Don’t cause or participate in any kind of fights or cause discomfort to anyone, neither mentally nor physically.
- Don’t create a false identity to confuse others.

# ⭐ General Guidelines

- Being a member of the kood/Jõhvi community means that all of us should aim to represent the school with the best intentions and messages as possible. That is what a community does.
- If you see anything inappropriate, someone not following the guidelines, or if something that bothered you happened, then please turn to the kood/Jõhvi team immediately.
- Talk to the kood/Jõhvi team about any problems, questions or misunderstandings that might occur. We will find a solution together.

# 🤹‍♀️ Event Guidelines

We’ve crafted some handy guidelines to ensure we’re all on the same page! 🤹‍♀️

**Official Events**: Keep an eye out for events posted by the kood/Jõhvi account. We’re jazzing up our posts with kood/ emojis to add a personalized touch—making it a breeze for you to follow.

**Event Calendar**: Official events are added to both Discord and Google Calendar, making it convenient for you to check out upcoming happenings. Here’s the event calendar link:

[https://www.notion.so/koodjohvi/Events-Calendar-a130842e51dc47cd9d7142ffe818474e](https://www.notion.so/koodjohvi/Events-Calendar-a130842e51dc47cd9d7142ffe818474e)

**Express Your Interest**: Planning to join an event? Hit that “interested” button on Discord! Your click helps us determine the number of participants, ensuring the best organization of events.

**Represent Well**: Good manners are always in style. Remember, you’re not just representing yourself—you’re a reflection of kood/Jõhvi. Keep those vibes positive! 🙂
