# Rules 📏

1. Be respectful. You must respect all users, regardless of your liking towards them. Treat others the way you want to be treated. 

2. Keep conversations in English.

3. Agree to disagree. 

4. No @mentioning spam. 

5. Do not organise, participate in, or encourage harassment of others. Disagreements happen and are normal, but continuous, repetitive, or severe negative comments may cross the line into harassment and are not okay. 

6. Do not organise, promote, or coordinate discussions around hate speech. It’s unacceptable to attack a person or a community based on attributes such as their race, ethnicity, national origin, sex, gender, sexual orientation, religious affiliation, or disabilities. 

7. Do not make threats of violence or threaten to harm others. This includes indirect threats, as well as sharing or threatening to share someone’s private personal information. 

8. Do not use offensive names and profile pictures. You will be asked to change your name or picture if the staff deems them inappropriate. 

9. Do not evade user blocks or server bans. Do not send unwanted, repeated friend requests or messages, especially after they’ve made it clear they don’t want to talk to you anymore. 

10. Do not send others viruses or malware, attempt to phish others, or hack.

11. kood/Jõhvi will Mute/Kick/Ban per discretion. If you feel mistreated DM a person with the

**@kood/Jõhvi team**

tag and we will resolve the issue. 

All Channels have descriptions explaining what they are there for and how everything works. If you don't understand something, feel free to ask! Your presence in this server implies accepting these rules, including all further changes. These changes might be done at any time without notice, it is your responsibility to check for them.
