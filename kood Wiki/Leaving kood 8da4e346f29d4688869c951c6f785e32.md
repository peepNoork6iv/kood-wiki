# Leaving //kood

Updated: March 16, 2026 11:15 AM

- **🥳 I have done all the mandatory tasks! What now?**
    
    Congratulations! We are very proud of you! If you haven’t done that yet, we encourage you to look for a job that meets your interests. 
    
    For more information on the Graduation Procedure visit [Graduation](Graduation%20241440b4458b800d8351f99098ec40f4.md) 
    
- **😖 I’m not sure //kood is for me… What shall I do?**
    
    Is there any hope that it still might be for you? We encourage you to speak with a team member to see if there’s any way we can support you and hopefully we can together come up what’s the best decision for you. 
    
- **🐾 I have decided I’m leaving. What do I have to do?**
    
    We are sorry to see you leave but we understand that life happens! In order to formalize your leaving, please send us an Early Termination Statement that is digitally signed stating that you wish to leave the school. Early Termination Statement form can be [found here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1BhqsP9uqWj1E-zYLt9DXSZ-l0t8axeMVmkDE1jvyjtE/edit?usp=sharing). Based on the moment you are leaving, an early termination fee may apply. Send digitally signed document to students@kood.tech.
    
    We wish you good luck with your future endeavors!

## Early Termination Fee

After getting accepted we have a Student Agreement that allows you to study and try the program with no fees for the first 3 months (Batch 1-4), Batch 5 - after 2 months, or after finishing the Coding Fundamentals module whichever is earlier.

*For Batch 1 - 2: the fee is distributed between months 4-24. For example, you decide to leave on month 6, that means you have to pay back 2x €200 = €400.* 

*For Batch 3 - 4 - If you leave after that then you need to pay an early termination fee that is counted according to the months studied in the program (months 4-14). For example, if you decide to leave on month 6, that means you have to pay back 3x €400 = €1200. If you leave in month 8, then you pay back 5 x €400 = €2000.* 

*For Batch 5-7: the fee is distributed between months 3-12. For example, you decide to leave on month 6, that means you have to pay back 4x €400 = €1600.*
