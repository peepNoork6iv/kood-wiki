# Study certificate

Updated: June 5, 2025 2:01 PM

If you wish to get the certificate proving your participation in the kood/Jõhvi process, please send an email to students@kood.tech with the following information:

- Full name
- Personal code / Document number
- Your Batch

*This certificate can be used for study leave from work and any other occasion where you might need to prove that you are an active student of kood/Jõhvi.*
