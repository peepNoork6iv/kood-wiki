# 2022.02 kood/Jõhvi update

With this, we are announcing a change to the current study curriculum and updating the student journey. Read through the description and the FAQ part to be up-to-date with your next steps as a student at kood/Jõhvi. 

[FAQ](https://app.notion.com/p/FAQ-f45efdaf0b904f1e9afe2c33a4105cc5?pvs=21) 

## **The mandatory program is 3 modules**

We had intensive talks with our partner companies and focus groups from current students. Based on their feedback, we made the decision that from now on the kood/Jõhvi 2-year program consists of 3 (2+1) compulsory modules + 1 optional module. The new program gives all the needed skills to have a successful career in the ICT sector. 

**Go** and **JavaScript** are compulsory modules that need to be completed, the third module can be **Rust** or **at least one specialisation module**. 

![Slaidshowle.png](2023%2002%20Update%20for%20Batch%201%20&%202%20(curriculum,%20studen/Slaidshowle.png)

In short, the new mandatory program expects you to complete **Go** and **JavaScript** + **Rust** or one **Specialisation module** (Branches)**.** Completing the mandatory program guarantees a student a **Diploma** upon graduation**.** 

**If you want to complete an optional module** (either Rust or a different specialisation) then you get an extra Certificate that showcases your acquired skills. You can complete different specialisations as some of them take less time than others and they are quite individual.

**Why the change?** The mandatory 2+1 program + kood/Talks and workshops gives a student all the skills needed to start their career in IT as a Junior Developer. 
This decision came from talking with our partner companies, developers, and current students based on the actual skills they have acquired from the program here at kood/Jõhvi and taking into account the needs of the local labor market.
If someone wants to finish the initial 2-year program that consists of 4 different modules then it is still possible and will only give the person more skills! 

More questions answered from here: **[FAQ](https://app.notion.com/p/FAQ-f45efdaf0b904f1e9afe2c33a4105cc5?pvs=21)** 

## **Student journey from start to the finish line**

**The student journey starts with Go.** Go programming language is the first mandatory module that needs to be completed by the start of the last JavaScript piscine.

**The 2nd module is JavaScript.** JavaScript is the 2nd mandatory module that needs to be completed.

**The 3rd module can be either Rust or at least one available Specialisation.** By completing the 3rd module you have officially completed the kood/Jõhvi program. You will get a Diploma from kood/Jõhvi and will be ready to hit the job market through our Talent network.

**The 4th module is optional.** This means that you finished the mandatory program and feel like you want to squeeze a bit more out of yourself. Well, go ahead and finish one more module! But do remember, you need to stay in the 2-year window in order to get a Certificate for the completed extra module from the school. 

- **Specialisation modules expected completion time**
    - Videogames: 6 months
    - BlockChain & Crypto: 2-week piscine + 4 months
    - Artificial Intelligence: 3-week piscine + 4/5 months
    - Mobile Applications: 2-week piscine + 5 months (21 weeks)
    - Cybersecurity: 5/6 months (22 weeks)
    - UI/UX: 4-week piscine (UX) + 3-week piscine (UI) + 4 months

If you feel like staying in the school for longer to complete extra modules, then write to us (info@kood.tech) and we’ll find a solution!

## **Graduation**

**For the 1st batch, the last day of studies is 12.09.2023.** After the last day of studies, you will be provided a digital Diploma but are also invited to the Graduation Ceremony.

**The Graduation Ceremony takes place 22.09.2023.** It is a celebratory occasion where founders, team members, and fellow graduates gather to commemorate the end of a significant chapter in your life. This special event marks the culmination of your hard work, dedication, and commitment.

**For the 2nd batch, the last day of studies is 20.11.2024.** 

**Finished the program early?** Good job! Your official graduation is 2 years after the start of your studies but we can provide all the documents regarding the end of studies earlier, if needed.  If you wish, you can continue with additional modules but otherwise, we encourage you to hunt for a new job! Our Talent Manager is ready to support you on this journey. 

**After graduation.** With graduating from kood/Jõhvi or after 2 years go by, you also need to free you dormitory spot, give back all equipment that was loaned by us and you will lose different extra perks (e.g. health insurance).

## Ending the Student Agreement

With the change in the program, the **2nd batch** has one extra month to make their decision on whether or not to end the Student Agreement. This means that the last day to end their Student Agreement without any Early Termination Fees is **21.03.2023**

If you want to leave the program after the trial period, you have to pay an Early Termination Fee. More info in the Student Agreement you signed available from [intra.kood.tech](https://intra.kood.tech/)

- **How to end the Student Agreement?**
    
    The  steps for ending the Student Agreement can be found in he Student Agreement paragraph 7. You can have a look via the intra ([intra.kood.tech](http://intra.kood.tech/)) --> left corner press on 'profile' and then you'll see the button to get the Agreement you signed. Take a look at the steps and act accordingly! 
    All in all it's a free form digitally signed letter stating your leave and the date + your name and addressed to "MTÜ Tuleviku Tehnoloogiaharidus"
    

# TL;DR

- The mandatory program for the 2-year program is being shortened from 4 modules to 3 modules.
- The compulsory modules are Go and JavaScript, and the third module can be Rust or one Specialisation module.
- Completing the mandatory program guarantees a student a Certificate of Completion, and completing an optional module results in an extra Certificate that showcases the student's acquired skills.
- Make sure to stick to all the [**Deadlines**](https://app.notion.com/p/2023-02-Update-curriculum-student-journey-recruitment-fees-c205c8a751a6431c832123e0ddaa6e62?pvs=21)
- The official date of graduation from the program is exactly 2 years (minus 1 day) from the start of studies, with specific dates provided for the 1st and 2nd batch.
- There are consequences for not meeting general deadlines, which can include loss of the dormitory spot, scholarships, or even the premature end of the Student Agreement.

In summary, the curriculum changes include a shortened mandatory program, a revised student journey, and specific graduation requirements and consequences for not meeting deadlines.
