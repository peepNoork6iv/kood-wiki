# Study program / curriculum

- **What are the mandatory modules in the kood/Jõhvi program?**
    
    The 2-year mandatory program consists of Go, JavaScript and Rust OR one Specialisation. Each language has mandatory tasks that need to be completed to advance to the next one.
    
    ![Slaidshowle.png](FAQ%20(1)/Slaidshowle.png)
    
- **What tasks do I need to complete?**
    
    First and foremost you need to complete all the **mandatory** **tasks** in the program. All other tasks are nice-to-have extras that will definitely give you more insight! Make sure to stay within the deadlines.
    
- **What is the third module that needs to be completed in the kood/Jõhvi program?**
    
    You can choose between **Rust** or one available **Specialisation** **module** (Video Games, Blockchain, Artificial Intelligence, Mobile Applications, Cyber Security + new branches in the future)
    
    After completing the 3rd module, a student has officially completed the kood/Jõhvi program.
    
- **What will a student receive upon completing the mandatory program?**
    
    Students who complete the mandatory program, will receive a Diploma. The Diploma showcases that a student has graduated by successfully completing their studies.
    
    The students are also strongly advised to find a job through our Talent network and put their skills to use. This will also benefit us, thanks to the partner company paying a recruitment fee for the Student, that finances the future growth of kood/Jõhvi.
    
- **What is the optional module in the kood/Jõhvi program?**
    
    When you have completed the mandatory program, you still have an optional module to complete, if you feel like it’s something that you want to do! 
    
    For this, you can start working on Rust or a Specialisation, dependent on what you chose as your 3rd module. When you finish an optional module, you will get an extra Certificate with your Diploma that showcases the learned skills.
    
- **Are there any benefits of completing an optional module in the kood/Jõhvi program?**
    
    Definitely! But all the benefits are for the person themselves as they can learn something new and interesting.
    
    Completing optional modules is also a useful advantage when applying for different jobs or positions. It also gives you a better understanding of working with different programs.
    
- **Can I do more than 3 modules?**
    
    Yes, of course! For any additional finished modules, we will provide an extra Certificate upon graduating. But do remember - you need to finish these module before the 2-year program ends.
    If something is left unfinished, you can always let us know and we can help with conditions to extend your studies
    
- **Why didn’t you tell me before? I would have chosen some other module.**
    
    We are sorry for the inconvenience. kood/Jõhvi’s first batch of students are pioneers in the Estonian education landscape - you have taken the leap and trusted this new study methodology. Our team is also on a journey to understanding what works best for you and constantly learning from you and our partners about what’s needed in the job market.
    
    We do hope that this solution gives you the opportunity to finish your study program within the 2-year time but also gives extra opportunities to do the full program in your free time. With this you can plan your time even better than before!

# Financials and recruitment fees

- **How does kood/Jõhvi operate financially?**
    
    kood/Jõhvi is a non-profit organization with a huge mission to decrease the lack of tech talents with no ambition to make any profit. We are offering this unique study opportunity, which currently is free for the students, however, each student costs us. Currently, it is all covered by donations from different organization / partner companies (thank you!) but we also have to have our own revenue streams in order to provide this type of education in the long run. 
    
    This means, that the recruitment fees come into play. Currently, we see that with a recruitment fee of €4000 per student, we are able to cover the main direct costs that the students (think: study platform license, hardware, campus maintenance and expenses, also the Selection Sprints, extra events, and the list goes on). In order to continue providing this type of education in the future, we need continuous help from organizations but also from our students to help to receive the recruitment fees.
    
- **Why do we have to pay to Termination Fees when not graduating?**
    
    Students who do not graduate are asked to pay the Termination Fee in order to cover the expenses that are made per student, explained in the Student Agreement. 
    
    You still have the option to be hired through our Talent network in order to avoid paying the Termination fee - this way the school will receive recruitment fees. It’s a win-win-win situation for all parties and the school can operate for years to come! 
    
- **I want to work for a non-partner company, how do I explain the recruitment fees?**
    
    We are preparing website that explains to all the recruiting companies how to hire from kood/Jõhvi. You can easily share more information via this page to your employer (page should be up in the beginning of March on [kood.tech](http://kood.tech) website). 
    
    We are also preparing a cover letter basis that explains the recruitment fee system - which you can simply copy into your cover letter. If needed, our team is also ready to speak with the recruiters that you are in contact with. It is a fee that is meant for the employer not the student.
    
- **Where do I find jobs that are paying recruitment fees?**
    
    The best way is to do so through our partners - we are collecting different roles and open positions to our [Discord](https://discord.com/channels/875324134530363395/1075346103643414568) and you can look for suitable positions, apply through us, we do a quick pre-screening, and then if the employer is interested in you we will make a direct introduction. 
    This way it is also guaranteed that the school will receive recruitment fee in order to be sustainable over time. You can always contact Liis / kood or liis@kood.tech with different recruitment questions
