## Temporary voice channels

You can create your own temporary voice channels by clicking the **#click to create** voice channel! 

<aside>
💡

### How to create a temporary voice channel:

1. navigate to the #voice channels category
2. find a #click to create channel
3. click on it
4. a new temporary voice channel is open for you!

</aside>

### How it works?

Temporary channels names follow the **#1 - Username's Channel** pattern. When you create a channel you become an admin of the channel and getting certain privileges, like:

- rights to re-name the channel
- slash commands for channel moderation

<aside>
💡

Temporary channels have **limitation of 20 people** at a time - if you feel like this has to be changed, please create a ticket 🎫

</aside>

### Why it is implemented?

We are experimenting with co-working possibilities on Discord, and main reasons for implementing this are:

- this function may help You to co-work with fellow students better
- it gives Students flexibility of creating as many voice channels as needed
- full control over them - voice channel creator is a voice channel owners and can set it up as they want
- voice channel removal - less anxiety about using public channels : )

### Ideas for usage:

- co-working space
- body doubling - studying with sound off and camera on
- code reviewing
- making your own master-classes for fellow students to help them : )

## Reaction Roles

<aside>
💡

### How to get a role:

1. Find a #reaction-roles channel
2. read posts there and leave reactions you think describe you best!
</aside>

### What is a reaction role?

Reaction role is a role, that you can pick for yourself by choosing an emoji under the message. Every emoji gives different roles, that are described in the message.

You can get yourself other roles besides your batch role! 

### Why would you get extra roles?

- we are using reactions to bring you more tailored notifications as we can tag each role separately, so we don’t have to abuse @everyone tag 😌
- for you to stay updated for the events in your area - there are plenty of things happening in Tallinn, Tartu and on-site
- to connect with people of your interests!

## Embedded messages

You are not affected by this function as your role permissions do not allow to use this function, but you might be noticing these messages around the server. For example, the #team-members channel
