# Minimum Income Threshold

Updated: March 31, 2026 5:05 PM

### **THE CONDITIONS AND PROCEDURE FOR CHANGING INCOME SHARING AGREEMENT MINIMUM INCOME THRESHOLD AT //KOOD**

In line with the terms set out in the Student Agreement (Section 3 and Annex 1), //kood (kood/Jõhvi, kood/Võru or kood/Järva) is implementing the following official policy regarding the Minimum Income Threshold used for Income Sharing Agreements (ISA).

This policy is governed by the original terms of the Student Agreement and is intended to provide clarity and procedural consistency. In case of any conflict, the signed Student Agreement takes precedence.

### 1. **THRESHOLD DEFINITION**

1.1. As defined in Annex 1 of the Student Agreement:

1.1.1. The Minimum Income Threshold is equal to the annual average gross monthly salary in Estonia, last published by Statistics Estonia, multiplied by a coefficient of 1.3 for Batch 3, Batch 4, Batch 5, Batch 6 and Batch 7 and multiplied with coefficient 1.2 for Batch 8 and onwards.

1.1.2. This value determines the income level above which the Student becomes liable to pay the Income Sharing Fee (according to ISA which is €4000).

1.2. The Minimum Threshold will be calculated using the following formula fixed in Annex 1:

1.2.1. As an example, 2024 average gross salary was €1,981 gross, meaning the Minimum Income Threshold was €1,981 × 1.3 = **€2,575.30.**

1.3. Based on the latest available gross salary data for 2025 by [**Statistikaamet](https://stat.ee/et/avasta-statistikat/valdkonnad/tooelu/palk-ja-toojoukulu/keskmine-brutokuupalk),** which is €2,092, the current threshold is €2,092 × 1.3 = **€2719.60** (Batch 3, Batch 4, Batch 5, Batch 6 and Batch 7) and €2,092 × 1.2 = **€2510.40** (Batch 8 and onwards).

### **2. ANNUAL THRESHOLD REVIEW AND FIXING PROCEDURE**

2.1. The Minimum Income Threshold will be reviewed and fixed once per calendar year.

2.2. The review is based on the annual average gross monthly salary published by Statistics Estonia for the previous calendar year.

2.3. The updated threshold becomes effective annually on May 1st.

2.4. The threshold remains in effect until April 30th of the following year.

2.5. The updated threshold is not retroactive and will apply only from its effective date onward.

### **3. NOTIFICATION PROCEDURE**

3.1. Students will be informed of the updated threshold at least one (1) month in advance, no later than April 1st each year.

3.2. The notification will be delivered via:

3.2.1. Official Discord announcement;

3.2.2. Email to the address registered in the Student Agreement.

3.3. Students are responsible for keeping their contact details up to date, including email. Failure to do so may result in missed communications and will not exempt them from compliance.

### **4. DURATION OF INCOME SHARING AGREEMENT AND REPORTING REQUIREMENTS**

4.1. The ISA remains valid for 10 years following graduation from the program. During this period, the Student must:

4.1.1. submit proof of income every 6 months (Batch 3, Batch 4, Batch 5, Batch 6 and Batch 7) and every 3 months (Batch 8 and onwards);

4.1.2. notify //kood of their first employer within one (1) month of Employment;

4.1.3. inform //kood in writing of any changes to their contact information, including email address via email [students@kood.tech](mailto:students@kood.tech). 

4.2. Acceptable documentation are tax returns, employer payslips, or other forms of verified income documentation, such as annual income declaration.

### 5. ISA PAYMENT CONDITIONS

5.1. The Student becomes liable for ISA payments if:

5.1.1. their monthly gross income (from any source) equals or exceeds the Minimum Income Threshold;

5.1.2. or their annual gross taxable income exceeds 12 × Minimum Income Threshold, regardless of employment status.

5.1.3. The ISA obligation applies based solely on income level, regardless of the industry, job title or role.

5.2. The repayment amount shall be a minimum of €200 monthly (Annex 1 of the Student Agreement).

5.3. Payments may be made by the Student or a third party (e.g., employer).

5.3.1. All such third-party payments must be communicated to and approved by //kood in writing.

5.4. The Student is entitled to repay the Income Sharing Fee in part or in full, at any time.

### **6. END OF OBLIGATION**

6.1. The obligation ends when the Student fully repays the agreed Income Sharing Fee in sum of €4000.

6.2. If the Student’s income has not met the Minimum Income Threshold during 10 years post-graduation, their ISA obligation will be considered automatically terminated, and no further payments are required.
