ℹ️ How to read the calendar

- [Placeholder] - the event time and place is not officially confirmed yet.
- PRIVATE - the event takes place in our building but is not for students.
- ONLINE - the event is for students but is taken place online.
- SC - the event is organized by Student Council
- SPRINTERS - the event is only meant for sprinters.
- PRE-REGISTERED - the event is meant for pre-registered students only.
- Be sure to check out the event location!
- Dormitory calendar is meant to see when we have outside people staying in the dormitory! (This includes sprinters and different bootcamp participants)

🤹‍♀️ Event Guidelines

We’ve crafted some handy guidelines to ensure we’re all on the same page! 🤹‍♀️

- **Official Events**: Keep an eye out for events posted by the kood/Jõhvi account. We’re jazzing up our posts with kood/ emojis to add a personalized touch—making it a breeze for you to follow.
- **Event Calendar**: Official events are added to both Discord and Google Calendar, making it convenient for you to check out upcoming happenings.
- **Express Your Interest**: Planning to join an event? Hit that “interested” button on Discord! Your click helps us determine the number of participants, ensuring the best organization of events.
- **Commitment Matters**: **Signed up — show up!** When you sign up for the event, it means that you’re committed to showing up as well. Skipping out means someone else misses the opportunity, and that’s not cool!
- **Represent Well**: Good manners are always in style. Remember, you’re not just representing yourself—you’re a reflection of kood/Jõhvi. Keep those vibes positive! 🙂

Let’s make these events memorable and build an awesome community together! 🎉 See you at the next event!
