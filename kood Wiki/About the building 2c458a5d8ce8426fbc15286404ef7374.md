# 👉 C**ommon Areas**

The school is built on 5 different floors with different objectives. These are the Lounge Area, Silent Area, Student Housing, and Co-working Area. School is open 24/7 (with a keycard), keep in mind that we have [Student Housing](Student%20housing%20guidelines%2085c516dbb0db4dba898ecf6c03629f20.md) in the same building, and if you wish to study at night, **be respectful towards others**.

- **1st floor** is a **Silent** & Lounge Area aka Community Space. We are hosting (community) events there and is a great place to hang out with fellow students. 1st floor includes a front desk, a meeting room, and a classroom.
- **2nd floor is Student Housing**. The entrance is for students who are living or renting a room there.
- **3rd floor is the Event Area**, which is meant for intense studying and focused work. Please keep that in mind and do not talk loudly or make noise here. There is also a meeting room and a lounge area (kitchen + couches). Bathrooms are located on both sides of the floor.
    - Most of the desks are **hot desks** - what does this mean? You have no ‘spot’ and this means that you have to clean up after working behind a certain station! So that the next person can use it if they wish to do so.
- The **4th floor is the Data Center**. 🚫 We do not use or have access to this part of the building. 🚫
- The **5th floor** is a great place for teamwork and catching up with your fellow students and you can find the Team Room there as well. Most of our events will take place there. There is also a meeting room and a lounge area (kitchen + couches). Bathrooms are located on both sides of the floor.
    - The hot desks rule applies here as well.

### 👉 Good to know:

## 👉 Meeting Rooms

We have 3 meeting rooms.

When using the meeting room, you are responsible for everything inside the room. The rooms have screens, tables and chairs. Use them responsibly.

- You have to clean the meeting rooms after you are done. Put things back and keep it tidy.
- Make sure to use the meeting rooms for their purpose - meetings, calls, etc. If you just wish to work in silence, use the Silent Floor (3rd floor) for that.

## 👉 Co-working Spaces

1. **Monitor Identification:**
    - Register your monitor with Peep to include a kood/Jõhvi sticker on it, so that others won’t take it.
2. **Cleanliness:**
    - Do not leave trash on your tables (empty plastic bottles, candy/snack wrappers & etc). Dispose of any waste appropriately.
    - No candles and perfume sprays are allowed in the co-working areas.
    - You are allowed to eat at your table but you have to clean up after yourself right after.
3. **Mugs and Cups:**
    - No dirty mugs, cups, or plates should be left unattended on tables. If left dirty or moldy, we reserve the right to dispose of them.
4. **Table Decoration:**
    - Personalize and decorate only your assigned table. Any exceptions require prior approval from a staff member.
5. **Table Usage:**
    - For permanent tables, individuals are allowed to use ONE table at a time.
6. **Table Checks**:
    - Regular table checks are conducted every 2 weeks. Non-compliance with guidelines, especially leaving a mess, may result in a notice and/or the school staff has a right to throw away the dirty items. Please follow those guidelines to maintain a clean and welcoming co-working space!

## ‼️The Don’ts ‼️

- Don't damage or deface kood/Jõhvi or its fixtures, décor or furnishings.
- Don’t break web, servers, devices or passwords or do other mean things.
- Don’t download or send anything that could break the Internet, or someone’s devices or property. Nor anything you wouldn’t want your mother to see.
- Don’t steal stuff! Using someone else’s physical or intellectual property is considered stealing. And stealing is a punishable crime. We will act accordingly.
- Don’t do drugs! The same goes with alcohol, especially within the school premises. It is **strictly prohibited** and police will be involved if needed.
- Don’t smoke/vape indoors or on the school premises. It has already been restricted in Estonia since 2005.
- Don’t act against the applicable laws, rules, and regulations of the Republic of Estonia.
- Don’t cause or participate in any kind of fights or cause discomfort to anyone, neither mental nor physical.
- Don’t create a false identity to confuse others.
