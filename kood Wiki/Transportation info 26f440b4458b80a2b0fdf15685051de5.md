# 🚗 By car

Paide is centrally located in Estonia and most main roads either go through the town or pass it very closely. Just add campus address to your maps and drive safely here. 

**Parking** is free in Paide and there are many options close to the campus. Just follow all the Estonian rules and park where possible (Rimi and Selver parking is the best, 5 min walk from campus).
**No-parking-zone** is in front of the campus. Please respect this.

## 🚌 By bus

There are several options to look for bus schedule, for example [www.tpilet.ee](http://www.tpilet.ee) or [www.luxexpress.eu](http://www.luxexpress.eu).

If there is direct route to Paide, then I suggest getting of at **Kesklinn, Paide**. This stop is 2 min walk from our campus. 

If you can’t find direct route to Paide, then I suggest looking two other stops - **Mäo** or **Mäeküla (Paide vald)**. Both are little further out from the center of Paide. Mäo stop is about 4 km and Mäeküla 11 km. If you choose to come to one of those stops, and need transport to Paide, please let **Kaspar** know on Discord or by [email](mailto:kaspar@kood.tech) and he will try to organize transport for you.

## 🚆 By train (Elron)

Train schedules and tickets can be purchased at [www.elron.ee](http://www.elron.ee). Train goes to **Türi**. which is about 15 km from Paide, but there is good local bus (free) connection between those two towns. Schedule can be found at [www.peatus.ee](http://www.peatus.ee) selcting **Türi bussijaam** as the stop where trains come and go and **Paide kesklinn** as a stop for coming to campus.
