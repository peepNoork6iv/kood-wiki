# Discord channels

Updated: November 11, 2025 10:15 AM

The //kood team and students use the server daily. Most of the school information students receive through Discord and all the communication with students (their questions, problems, etc.) is also going through here. ✉️ **All the kood/Jõhvi team members are available on Discord**, so it’s possible to contact any of the team members directly. 

Channels are divided into groups, so it is easier to browse between them. Overall groups can also be divided into studying-connected ones and leisure-time ones. 

---

Here is the exact structure of the kood/Jõhvi server with explanations; channels and channel groups are clickable. ⬇️

- announcements
    
    Here are all the major announcements being posted. Keep the notifications on for this channel!
    
- welcome
    
    Is used as a welcome-page for students, who are not server-members yet; contains welcome words and info on how to enter the server
    
- server-rules
    
    Contains rules on how to act in the server, so you are not getting kicked 😄
    
- reaction-roles
    
    Powered by bot, this channel assigns roles based on reactions. For example, if the students chooses cybersecurity as their specialisation, then they can leave dedicated emoji and become a part of the group. Groups make communication easier, as we can mention certain group, and so address people more specifically.
    

### Info Board

This channel-group is dedicated to the school communication with students, where we post a different kind of studying/work/team-related announcements

- events
    
    Different events that are happening both in and out of the school building.
    
- team-members
    
    Get to know the kood/Jõhvi team and their area of responsibilities.
    
- faq-on-kood
    
    Frequently asked questions and we'll try to answer them! If you know how to help, you can answer the questions as well.
    
- work-and-internships
    
    Channel to send and spread different work or internship-related content.
    
- offers
    
    Offers channel for sharing offers: volunteering opportunities, events and etc for kood/Jõhvi students.
    
- leaving-campus
    
    Here students can leave a notice if they are leaving the campus
    
- batch_X-info
    
    Channel with info for the Batch 1/2/3/4 only.
