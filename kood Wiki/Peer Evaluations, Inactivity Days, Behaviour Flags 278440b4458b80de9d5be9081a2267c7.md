# Peer Evaluations, Inactivity Days, Behaviour Flags and Strikes

Updated: September 24, 2025 2:38 PM

### **THE CONDITIONS AND PROCEDURE FOR PEER EVALUATIONS, INACTIVITY DAYS, BEHAVIOUR FLAGS AND STRIKES**

This academic policy encapsulates the core academic operational guidelines of //kood. Adherence to these policies is expected of all students to maintain a fair, structured and high-quality learning experience. This policy is subject to updates, which will be communicated accordingly.

- **1. ACADEMIC ETHICS**
    
    Students have a responsibility to present work that is truly their own. Cheating, plagiarism or misrepresenting the work of others is a serious offense that can result in termination of the student contract by the //kood team.
    
    All study materials (including, but not limited to exams, tasks, group works, Discord server conversations and other intellectual property) related to //kood or Study Platform must
    
    **not be**
    
    copied, distributed or shared with other community members or third parties.
    
- **2. PEER EVALUATIONS**
    
    2.1. At //kood, we believe in the power of collaborative learning and the importance of peer feedback. As part of our commitment to fostering a supportive and interactive educational environment, peer evaluations through code review systems are integral to our academic processes.
    
    2.1.1. Evaluations are conducted in groups of 3–6 people, depending on the project.
    
    2.1.2. The full review process must be completed within 7 days.
    
    2.1.3. Each reviewer has to check in within 72 hours and start the review process on the task review page.
    
    2.1.4. Students must report planned inactivity at least 4 days in advance.
    
    2.2. Each student has 42 inactivity days available for the duration of their studies. During inactivity days, students will not be assigned new reviews.
    
    2.2.1. Minimum inactivity period is 4 days.
    
    2.2.2. Inactivity must be scheduled at least 1 week before the start date and cannot be modified afterwards.
    
    2.2.3. The student will be excluded from the review process, effective beginning nine (9) days prior to the start of the designated inactivity period.
    
    2.3. For student to access inactivity days:
    
    2.3.1. Student clicks their profile;
    
    2.3.2. Student clicks on *Inactivity tab*;
    
    2.3.3. Student can click the *Add inactivity period* button to add inactive days.
    
    2.4. In cases of illness or other serious circumstances, students must notify study administration as soon as possible via [students@kood.tech](mailto:students@kood.tech).
    
- **3. BEHAVIOUR FLAGS**
    
    3.1 A behaviour flag is a system-generated or peer-reported indicator of conduct that does not meet review and feedback participation requirements. Flags serve as early warnings to give students a chance to improve. Types of Behaviour Flags are:
    
    **3.2. FAILED TO CHECK IN**
    
    3.2.1. Meaning: Failure to check in during the first review phase.
    
    3.2.2. Trigger: No check-in within 3 days of the 7-day window.
    
    3.2.3. Consequence: Written warning on first occurrence.
    
    **3.3. REVIEW WAS LEFT UNFINISHED**
    
    3.3.1. Meaning: Review process not completed.
    
    3.3.2. Trigger: Required steps (meeting, notes, approvals, tests) not completed by deadline.
    
    3.3.3. Consequence: After 3 occurrences, written warning (appeals possible).
    
    **3.4. FEEDBACK DEADLINE MISSED**
    
    3.4.1. Meaning: Failure to submit 360 feedback on time.
    
    3.4.2. Consequence: After 3 missed deadlines, may result in a written warning.
    
    **3.5. PROBLEMATIC FEEDBACK**
    
    3.5.1. Meaning: Repeatedly poor or concerning 360 feedback results.
    
    3.5.2. Trigger: Low ratings (1–2 stars) with aligned comments across multiple reviewers.
    
    3.5.3. Consequence: After 3 flagged instances, investigation is launched. If confirmed, written warning is issued (appeals possible).
    
    **3.6. REPORT**
    
    3.6.1. Meaning: Misconduct reported by another student during review.
    
    3.6.2. Trigger: Inactivity despite check-in, refusal to participate, stalling, or disrespectful behaviour.
    
    3.6.3. Consequence: Written warning on first occurrence (appeals possible).
    
    3.7. Check-in and report must be at least 72 hours apart to be valid.
    
- **4. STRIKES**
    
    4.1 A strike is a pre-warning assigned for non-participation in reviews.
    
    4.2 Strike Rules:
    
    4.2.1. Non-participation without notification results in a grade of X.
    
    4.2.2. Three strikes = one written warning.
    
    4.2.3. Three repeated non-participations (3 × X) result in termination of studies.
    
    4.3 Students are notified of strikes via platform messages or email, with reminders about the importance of peer reviews.
    
- **5. CONSEQUENCES OF NON-PARTICIPATION**
    
    5.1. One written warning is equal to a formal academic notice.
    
    5.2. Multiple warnings lead to termination of the Student Agreement.
    
    5.3. Termination occurs after repeated non-participation (three ‘X’ grades) or persistent misconduct.
    
- **6. DEADLINES**
    
    6.1. Each batch has its own deadlines, detailed in Student Agreements.
    
    6.2. All mandatory tasks must be completed on time.
    
    6.3. Failure to meet deadlines may result in:
    
    6.3.1. loss of scholarships, and/or;
    
    6.3.2. termination of the Student Agreement (early termination fees will apply).
    
- **7. APPEALS**
    
    7.1. Students may appeal any flag, strike or written warning by providing evidence (messages, timestamps, deliverables) to [students@kood.tech](mailto:students@kood.tech). A Study Appeal template is provided [here.](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1OTQzPp1mLUZyPn1ol2SYhi3DgGLZNmg8nFhHhhHbdwI/edit?usp=sharing)
    
    7.2. Appeals are reviewed by the //kood team and unjustified flags, strikes and written warning may be cleared.
