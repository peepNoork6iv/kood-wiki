# 👉 C**ommon Areas**

Our new learning centre rooms are now open, you can come and go 24/7. Our rooms are located on the 3rd floor. There is one study area with 30 seats for now, and two quiet booths. Magnetic cards will open the doors, if you don’t have one, please contact community manager Kaspar. If you wish to study at night, **be respectful towards others**.

- **1st floor** hosts our friendly Coop store. It’s open from 08:00 - 20:00 on weekdays and 10:00-20:00 on weekends.
- **2nd floor is offices of** [Järvamaa Development Agency](https://jarva.ee/) and [coWork Paide](https://coworkpaide.ee/).
- **3rd floor is for kood/Järva.** There is one big study area, a kitchen and a eating place, some nice couch areas (coming 2026) etc.
- **4th floor is common area** with one big meetingroom and large balcony area. You can use the balcony area for free, if you don’t disturb ongoing meetings that are happening indoors.

## 👉 Student Housing

These guidelines are meant for students who live in our student housing. Please read them and if you have any questions, please turn to the team! [Student housing guidelines ](Student%20housing%20guidelines%2085c516dbb0db4dba898ecf6c03629f20.md) 

## 👉 Study Space

1. **Monitor Identification:**
    - Register your monitor with Kaspar to include a kood/Jõhvi sticker on it, so that others won’t take it.
2. **Cleanliness:**
    - Do not leave trash on your tables (empty plastic bottles, candy/snack wrappers & etc). Dispose waste appropriately.
    - No candles and perfume sprays are allowed!
    - You are allowed to eat at the kitchen area and **NOT** at ****your table. Small snacking is allowed. Please clean up after yourself right after.
3. **Mugs and Cups:**
    - No dirty mugs, cups, or plates should be left unattended on tables. If left dirty or moldy, we reserve the right to dispose of them. Dirty cups and other utensils must be put into dishwasher which is operated by our cleaning personell.
4. **Table Decoration:**
    - Personalize and decorate only your assigned table. Any exceptions require prior approval from the team of //kood.
5. **Table Usage:**
    - For permanent tables, individuals are allowed to use ONE table at a time.
6. **Table Checks**:
    - Regular table checks are conducted every 2 weeks. Non-compliance with guidelines, especially leaving a mess, may result in a notice and/or the school staff has a right to throw away the dirty items. Please follow those guidelines to maintain a clean and welcoming co-working space!

## ‼️The Don’ts ‼️

- Don't damage or deface kood/Järva or its fixtures, décor or furnishings.
- Don’t break web, servers, devices or passwords or do other mean things.
- Don’t download or send anything that could break the Internet, or someone’s devices or property. Nor anything you wouldn’t want your mother to see.
- Don’t steal stuff! Using someone else’s physical or intellectual property is considered stealing. And stealing is a punishable crime. We will act accordingly.
- Don’t do drugs! The same goes with alcohol, especially within the school premises. It is **strictly prohibited** and police will be involved if needed.
- Don’t smoke/vape indoors or on the school premises, this includes the terrace. It has already been restricted in Estonia since 2005.
- Don’t act against the applicable laws, rules, and regulations of the Republic of Estonia.
- Don’t cause or participate in any kind of fights or cause discomfort to anyone, neither mental nor physical.
- Don’t create a false identity to confuse others.
