# Ticketing system

Updated: January 21, 2025 9:45 AM

Hey hey!
We have added a new feature to the server - ticketing system! The main idea of this feature is to enhance the way we manage support requests and improve your overall communication experience. 

It is operated by the 3rd party Tickets bot → [read more](https://ticketsbot.net/)

<aside>
💡 How to open a ticket:

1. Navigate to the **Support** section on Discord server
2. Find the **#open-a-ticket** channel (use ”Browse channels”)
3. Press “**Open a ticket**”
4. Fill in the form detailing your problem
5. Wait for a response (Initial response time is one working day)!

</aside>

## Why are we implementing a ticketing system?

We’re making this change to provide a more structured, efficient and transparent way to handle your questions and issues. These are some of the main reasons:

- **Improved Tracking** - The ticketing system lets us monitor and track all issues from start to finish. This means we can analyze how long it takes to resolve issues and identify any areas where we can improve our response times.
- **Transparency** - you will actually see who handles your ticket
- **Feedback** - you have the opportunity of rating your satisfaction with the help received, so we can grow and improve based on your needs
- **Centralized Support** - By moving all support requests to one place, it will reduce the load on specific team member as other can help as well. I addition you as a student don’t have to think about who to write with a specific issue as all of kood/Jõhvi team members will be able to see the issues and claim the requests.

## What kinds of problems I can bring up via tickets?

You can use the ticketing system for **any issue** that you would typically discuss with the kood/Support account or any team member via direct messages. Whether it’s a technical issue, a complaint, or a general question, the ticketing system is your go-to for all support needs.
Some examples: 

- Platform configurations
- Questions about the curriculum
- Server or internet questions
- Advice or suggestions about the studies
- Platform bugs

## How my ticket is being handled?

When you submit the ticket, someone from the team will claim the ticket. When the issue is resolved, you can either close the ticket yourself or we will send you a closing ticket request, so that you can decide to close it by yourself. 

All tickets are **private**, meaning that others do not see your question. But you can add people if you think someone you know could benefit from the question you ask - your teammates for example!

## The ticket is closed - what’s next?

You will receive a message from the Tickets bot with the transcript of the ticket, as the actual ticket is being removed once closed. 
In this message you will see the info about who handled your ticket, when it was open, who closed the ticket, and a link to the ticked transcript. 
You can rate your satisfaction with the help received by choosing between 1-5 stars under the message.
