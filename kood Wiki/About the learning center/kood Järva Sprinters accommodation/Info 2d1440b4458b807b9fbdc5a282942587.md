Since our own domitory is still waiting to be built, we must accommodate our sprinters throughout the whole city - there are three spaces in use under the name of “Hostel.” 
Here’s everything you need to know: 👇

## Paide Sportscenter Hostel

⚽ Our biggest accommodation parter - 30 sprinters will get their quality sleep there. They have rooms for 3-6 people, shared showers and toilets, a kitchen and a common area with some games.
📍 Location: Aiavilja 1, Paide linn (Paide E-Piim Spordihall) ([Google Maps link](https://maps.app.goo.gl/KkgbvLHxKiRh1rKb9)).

## Järvamaa Huntersclub Hostel

🦊 They are our new partners and will host 9 sprinters. Three rooms with 2-beds and one room with 3 beds. There is shared bathroom, seperate toilets and shared kitchen with public space.
📍 Location: Jahi tn. 1, Paide linn ([Google Maps link](https://maps.app.goo.gl/TVWo7WPp7S1S79Wx7)).
