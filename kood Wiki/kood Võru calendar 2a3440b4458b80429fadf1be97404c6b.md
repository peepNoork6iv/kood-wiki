# **Key to calendar symbols**

👩‍💼 **Individual visit -** A single guest or visitor at kood/Võru

🚀 **Development programme -** Events related to the entrepreneurship or growth programme

🎓**Education event (external) -** Education-related activities outside the school and everyone can take part! 

🏛️ **In-house event (non-disruptive) -** Events at kood/Võru that don't affect learners

 **👨‍🎓Learner-impacting visit/event -** Guests or activities at school that may affect the learning environment

🪩 **Event / celebration at school** – festive or large-scale event taking place at kood/Võru (may cause major disturbance to classes)

👨‍💻 **Office Hours** - Takes place on every Tuesday on site or on Discord. Good opportunity to ask questions and get help with your projects!
