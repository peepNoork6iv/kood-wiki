### IMPORTANT INFO

<aside>
💡

The deadline for changing the module is **one month** after the start of the module

- During the 1st month you can try several modules at the same time

After 1 month trial:

- You can only do **one module**
- The decision is final.

If you complete the module before the deadline then this date is the date of your studies completion.

</aside>
